# About Me:
Hi, I’m Ansar! I’m a 3rd-year Software Systems Engineering student at the University of Regina. I enjoy building software that solves real-world problems and makes everyday tasks a bit easier. I’m always curious and constantly learning—whether it's through hands-on projects, collaborating with others, or diving into new technologies. I really value teamwork and the process of turning ideas into working solutions, especially when it means learning something new along the way.

Outside of academics, I’m someone who values balance. I love traveling and discovering new places, spending time with friends, and unwinding with a good gaming session. Whether I’m writing code, brainstorming with a team, or just enjoying the little things in life, I’m always looking to grow, connect, and create something meaningful.
